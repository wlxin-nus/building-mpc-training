## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-13

### Added

- Standalone hierarchical building MPC identification, refit, validation, and model-freezing code.
- Frozen case profiles and model artifacts for SZ Air, MZ Air, and MZ Hydronic.
- Offline tests, dependency lock, provenance manifest, and registered protocol documents.
- English publication and contribution metadata.
- Cross-platform continuous integration for the supported Python versions.
- Security, citation, checksum, and third-party dependency guidance.

### Changed

- Extracted the MPC workflow from the larger H3C research codebase without changing its numerical
  model, optimization settings, case configuration, or frozen coefficients.
